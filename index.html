How hybrid works Short reports on-device; long threads and security issues get the full report
  1. Short reports start on-device. Gemini Nano runs in your browser, so the text never leaves your device.
  2. Long threads go to the cloud. Anything over 1,200 characters is answered by Gemini Flash in the cloud with a full report. A short report that looks like a security issue gets the full report too — on-device when Gemini Nano is available, otherwise in the cloud.
  3. Every result names its engine. The badge on each result says On-device · Gemini Nano or Cloud · Gemini Flash, with the latency.

On-device needs Chrome Desktop 139+ with Gemini Nano downloaded; on other machines everything runs in the cloud and the badge says so. In our live tests, quick triage answered in about 1–2 seconds via the cloud.
